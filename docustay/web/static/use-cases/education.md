# Permission slips and school forms

Send a permission slip to every family at once. Each parent signs from a phone and you see who is still missing.

## Flip through the pages first.

Each card is the real document: its pages, its fields, its wording.

- [Permission slip template](/templates/permission-slip-template)
- [Photo release form](/templates/photo-release-form)
- [Volunteer agreement template](/templates/volunteer-agreement-template)

## Three documents for a school year.

**Permission slip.** A parent agrees to a trip or an activity.

**Photo release.** Permission to use a child's picture.

**Volunteer agreement.** Terms for an adult who helps out.

## How it goes.

For example, a teacher sends a permission slip to every family from a list, and checks that evening who has not yet signed.

1. **Send to a list**: Every family at once
2. **Check**: Who has signed
3. **Parent signs**: On a phone
4. **Keep**: A record each

## A copy you can stand behind.

**Sealed copy.** One PDF with every signature, sealed so a later change shows.

**Certificate.** Who signed, when and from where, on the last page.

**Audit log.** Each step in order, exportable as a CSV.

## A parent signs for one child, once.

The parent or guardian fills in the details, ticks what they allow and signs.


## Every family, from one list.

Send the permission slip to the class list. Each family fills it in on their own phone and gets their own sealed copy.

**Saved.** Names and emails join your People list for the next trip.

## The email is in the school's name.

The email goes out under the school's name, with its logo and its reply-to address.

**Reminders.** Reminders go out until each family has signed.

## Documents for trips and activities.

Built to be sent to a whole class or a whole club in one go.

**Permission slip template.** A parent agrees to a trip or an activity. Parent or guardian signs, over 2 pages and 20 fields, about 8 minutes in all.

**Volunteer agreement template.** Terms for an adult who helps without pay. Organization and Volunteer each sign their own boxes, over 3 pages and 23 fields, about 7 minutes in all.

## What the document asks for.

Read from the template itself, so this list is what the signer will see.

**What the permission slip asks for.** School or group, Trip or activity, Date, Child’s full name, Child’s date of birth, Class or year, Health notes, My child will bring medicine, Dietary needs, Emergency contact’s name, Their phone, My phone, If I cannot be reached, I agree that the adults in charge may get urgent medical help for my child, How the group travels, and 6 more. Each box sits where it belongs on the page and belongs to one person, so nobody fills in a part that is not theirs.

**What the volunteer agreement asks for.** Organization name, Organization’s legal name, Volunteer’s full name, Role, Start date, Hours or schedule, Supervisor’s name, Expenses covered, if any, Emergency contact’s name, Their phone, Printed name, Volunteer signature, Volunteer date signed, Title or capacity, and 9 more. Each box sits where it belongs on the page and belongs to one person, so nobody fills in a part that is not theirs.

## Before you send it home.

A permission slip works best when it is specific and arrives early.

**Say what, when and who to call.** A good slip names the activity, the date and a person to call. The template has a place for each, so a parent can answer in one read.

**One list for the whole class.** Upload the class list and send the slip to every family at once. Each parent gets their own copy and signs it on their own phone.

## While the slips come back.

The question every teacher asks is who has not answered yet.

**No paper to chase.** A parent opens the email, signs with a finger and is done. There is no account, no app and nothing to print or send back in a bag.

**See the gaps.** The document list shows who has signed and when, so you can follow up with the families who have not answered.

## Keeping the record.

A signed slip is the answer to a question you may be asked months later.

**A sealed copy for each child.** Each signed slip is its own sealed PDF with a certificate of completion, so one family's answer is easy to find.

**An export for the office.** The audit log exports as a CSV, so the school office can keep the list with the trip file.

**Free to start.** A document counts when it is sent, and the Free plan includes 15 documents a month, with a list send of up to 15 at a time.

## Questions

**Can I send to a whole class?**

Yes, from a spreadsheet, on every plan. Free sends to up to 15 at a time.

**Can both parents sign?**

Yes. Add each as a signer.

**Can I see who has not signed?**

Yes. The document list shows each person's status, and a reminder goes out by hand or on a schedule.
