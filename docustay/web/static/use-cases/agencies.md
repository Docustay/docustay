# Statements of work and client agreements

Send the scope, then each change to it, then the payment plan. Clients sign and pay in the same sitting.

## Flip through the pages first.

Each card is the real document: its pages, its fields, its wording.

- [Statement of work template](/templates/statement-of-work-template)
- [Change order template](/templates/change-order-template)
- [Service agreement template](/templates/service-agreement-template)

## Four documents for a client job.

**Statement of work.** What will be done, by when, for how much.

**Change order.** A signed change to the scope or the price.

**Payment plan agreement.** Payments by date, collected as it is signed.

## How it goes.

For example, an agency sends a statement of work with a deposit field, and the client signs and pays in the same sitting.

1. **Scope**: Statement of work
2. **Sign**: Client signs
3. **Pay deposit**: Inside the document
4. **Changes**: Change orders

## A copy you can stand behind.

**Sealed copy.** One PDF with every signature, sealed so a later change shows.

**Certificate.** Who signed, when and from where, on the last page.

**Audit log.** Each step in order, exportable as a CSV.

## Questions

**Can the client pay when they sign?**

Yes. Add a payment field. It goes to your own Stripe account.

**Can I put my own branding on it?**

Yes. Your logo and colours appear on the document and the emails.

**Can I send through the API?**

Yes. See Sending from your code.
