# HR paperwork and onboarding

Send the offer, then the first-day paperwork, to each new person as a package they can sign in one sitting.

## Flip through the pages first.

Each card is the real document: its pages, its fields, its wording.

- [Offer letter template](/templates/offer-letter-template)
- [New hire paperwork](/templates/new-hire-paperwork)
- [Non-solicitation agreement](/templates/non-solicitation-agreement)

## Three documents for a new person.

**Offer letter.** Role, pay and start date, for the candidate to accept.

**New hire paperwork.** The forms to fill on day one, in one send.

**To a whole group.** Send to a list from a spreadsheet on Pro.

## How it goes.

For example, a shop sends an offer letter and the first-day paperwork together, and the new hire signs both from a phone the night before they start.

1. **Offer**: Offer letter
2. **Accept**: Candidate signs
3. **Paperwork**: First-day forms
4. **File**: Sealed copies

## A copy you can stand behind.

**Sealed copy.** One PDF with every signature, sealed so a later change shows.

**Certificate.** Who signed, when and from where, on the last page.

**Audit log.** Each step in order, exportable as a CSV.

## Questions

**Can the candidate sign on a phone?**

Yes. No app and no account.

**Can a manager approve first?**

Yes. Add an approver before the candidate.

**Is anything sent for me?**

No. Nothing goes out until you press send.
