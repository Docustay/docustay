# Leases and rental applications

Collect the application, then send the addendum and the deposit agreement. The tenant signs first and the landlord is told when it is their turn.

## Three documents for a rental.

**Rental application.** The applicant fills it in and signs.

**Lease addendum.** A change to the lease, signed in order.

**Deposit agreement.** The deposit, its terms and when it comes back.

## How it goes.

For example, a landlord sends a lease addendum, the tenant signs first, and the landlord is told it is their turn.

1. **Apply**: Rental application
2. **Sign**: Lease addendum
3. **Pay deposit**: Inside the document
4. **Keep**: Sealed copy

## A copy you can stand behind.

**Sealed copy.** One PDF with every signature, sealed so a later change shows.

**Certificate.** Who signed, when and from where, on the last page.

**Audit log.** Each step in order, exportable as a CSV.

## Landlord and Tenant, in order.

The Landlord describes the change. The Tenant signs first, and the Landlord is told when it is their turn.


## From the application to the kept copy.

**Send the application.** The applicant fills in the rental application and signs from their phone.

**Sign the addendum.** The tenant signs first. The landlord is told when it is their turn.

**Pay the deposit.** The deposit is a payment field inside the agreement, charged through your own Stripe account.

**Keep the record.** Both get the sealed PDF and a certificate.

## Applications and addenda for property work.

The paperwork that passes between a landlord, a manager and the person who rents.

**Rental application form.** The applicant fills it in and signs. Applicant signs, over 4 pages and 43 fields, about 18 minutes in all.

**Lease addendum template.** A change to a lease, signed in order. Landlord and Tenant each sign their own boxes, over 3 pages and 24 fields, about 9 minutes in all.

**Deposit agreement.** The deposit, its terms and when it comes back. Business and Customer each sign their own boxes, over 3 pages and 18 fields, about 7 minutes in all.

## What the document asks for.

Read from the template itself, so this list is what the signer will see.

**What the rental application form asks for.** Property address, Move-in date, Monthly rent, if listed, Full name, Date of birth, Phone, Email, Current address, How long at this address, Current landlord’s name and phone, Why you are moving, How many, including you, Other occupants, Pets, and 29 more. Each box sits where it belongs on the page and belongs to one person, so nobody fills in a part that is not theirs.

**What the lease addendum asks for.** Property address, Date of this addendum, Landlord’s name, Tenant’s name, Date of the original lease, What it is about, Write the new terms, Effective date, Type, breed and size, Pet’s name, Pet deposit or fee, in US dollars, Extra amount, in US dollars, When it is due, Space number or area, and 10 more. Each box sits where it belongs on the page and belongs to one person, so nobody fills in a part that is not theirs.

**What the deposit agreement asks for.** Date of this agreement, Business name, Customer’s full name, What the deposit holds, Pay deposit, How the deposit is held, Refund within, the condition for a refund, Date after which the deposit is kept, Remaining balance and when it is due, Governing law (state), Printed name, Title or capacity, Business signature, and 4 more. Each box sits where it belongs on the page and belongs to one person, so nobody fills in a part that is not theirs.

## Before you send it to a tenant or applicant.

Two different documents, two different ways of sending.

**An application goes to one applicant.** Send the rental application to each applicant, who fills in their details and signs from a phone. Each application is saved on its own, so you are not sorting a shared inbox.

**An addendum goes to named people.** A lease addendum goes to the tenants on a lease, each by email, with the signing order you choose. Put the names and addresses in before sending so they only have to read and sign.

## While tenants sign.

People sign at home in the evening, so make it easy on a phone.

**Sign from the sofa.** The signing page works on a phone with no app and no account, and the signer can draw a signature with a finger.

**Reminders until it is back.** Reminders go out automatically until everyone on the lease has signed.

**Collect a fee in the same step.** A payment field can take an application fee or a deposit as the last step of signing, through your own Stripe account. Paying comes after the signature.

## After the lease is signed.

A lease runs for a year or more, so the record matters.

**Payments in one list.** Every payment taken while signing appears on the Payments page with who paid, for which document, and its status.

**A record for every step.** The audit log shows when each tenant received, opened and signed, which settles most questions about who saw what and when.

## Questions

**Can a deposit be paid inside the document?**

Yes. Add a payment field. It goes to your own Stripe account.

**Can two tenants sign?**

Yes. Add each as a signer, in order or together.

**Is a lease template included?**

Not yet. A full lease is not in the library today, but you can upload your own lease and add the signing boxes.
