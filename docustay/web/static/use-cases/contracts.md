# Contracts that get signed

Send an agreement and let everyone sign from a link. You keep one sealed copy and the record of who signed when.

## Three agreements people send most.

**Service agreement.** Scope, fees and term between a provider and a client.

**Independent contractor.** Who does the work, how it is paid, and who owns the result.

**Vendor agreement and SOW.** Terms for a supplier, then a statement of work for each job.

## How it goes.

For example, a studio sends a services agreement to a client on Monday, the client signs on a phone that afternoon, and both sides have the sealed PDF before the first invoice.

1. **Draft**: Template or Scribe
2. **Send**: Link by email
3. **Sign**: Any device
4. **Keep**: Sealed copy + record

## A copy you can stand behind.

**Sealed copy.** One PDF with every signature, sealed so a later change shows.

**Certificate.** Who signed, when and from where, on the last page.

**Audit log.** Each step in order, exportable as a CSV.

## Two parties, eight pages, every field placed.

The Provider and the Client each have their own fields. Initials go on every page, and the signatures and dates are at the end.


## One agreement, four steps.

**Place or keep the fields.** Start from the template with its fields in place, or change them.

**Send it.** The email names who asked and carries one button.

**They sign.** On a phone or a computer, with no account.

**You both keep it.** A sealed PDF with a certificate of who signed and when.

## Check these before an agreement goes out.

Most delays come from a missing name, a wrong date or the wrong person receiving it.

**Who signs, and in what order.** Decide whether the signers go one at a time or all at once. One at a time suits an agreement the client reviews after you have signed; all at once is quicker when nothing depends on order.

**Fields only you can fill.** Some boxes, such as your own name and the date of the agreement, are filled in by you before sending, so the other side never has to. The template marks them as yours.

## While the agreement is out.

You can see where it stands without asking anyone.

**See who has opened it.** Each document shows whether it was sent, opened or signed, and by whom. If a signer has not opened it, you know before you chase them.

**Let reminders do the chasing.** Reminders go out on their own until everyone has signed, so a forgotten email does not hold up the start date.

**Signers need no account.** The signer opens the link on a phone or a computer, reads, draws or types a signature and finishes. There is nothing to install and no password.

## Once it is signed, it is findable.

The record does not end at the last signature.

**Everyone keeps a copy.** Both parties receive the sealed PDF with its certificate. You can download it again from the document at any time.

**Send the same agreement again.** Save your own copy of the template with your wording and fields, and the next client starts from that. Free makes up to 5 new templates a month; the paid plans have no limit.

## Questions

**Can I change the wording?**

Yes. Your copy of a template is yours to edit before you send it. Nothing in it is legal advice.

**Can both sides sign in order?**

Yes. Set the provider to sign first, or let both sign at once.

**Can Scribe draft one that is not listed?**

Describe a different one and Scribe writes it; you review it before it is sent.
