# Waivers and consent forms

Share one link before an event or a visit. Each person signs on their own phone and you have a record for every one.

## Flip through the pages first.

Each card is the real document: its pages, its fields, its wording.

- [Liability waiver template](/templates/liability-waiver-template)
- [Photo release form](/templates/photo-release-form)
- [Media release form](/templates/media-release-form)

## Five forms people collect most.

**Liability waiver.** Risk and responsibility, signed before an activity.

**Photo and media release.** Permission to use pictures, recordings and names.

**Permission slip and medical consent.** A parent or guardian agrees; care is authorised.

## How it goes.

For example, a climbing gym shares one waiver link at the front desk, each guest signs on their own phone, and the gym can find any signed waiver by name.

1. **Share**: One link
2. **Sign**: On their phone
3. **Saved**: A record each
4. **Find**: By name

## A copy you can stand behind.

**Sealed copy.** One PDF with every signature, sealed so a later change shows.

**Certificate.** Who signed, when and from where, on the last page.

**Audit log.** Each step in order, exportable as a CSV.

## Questions

**Do people need an account?**

No. They open the link and sign.

**Can one link serve a whole group?**

Yes. A link form gives each person their own signed copy.

**Where do the signed forms go?**

Into your document list and People list, each with its certificate.
