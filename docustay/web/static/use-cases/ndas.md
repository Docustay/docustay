# NDAs signed in minutes

Send a non-disclosure agreement before the first conversation. Both sides sign from a link, and both keep the sealed copy.

## Flip through the pages first.

Each card is the real document: its pages, its fields, its wording.

- [NDA template](/templates/nda-template)
- [Non-solicitation agreement](/templates/non-solicitation-agreement)

## Two documents for the start of a deal.

**NDA.** Both parties promise to keep what they share private.

**Non-solicitation agreement.** A promise not to hire away each other's people.

**In your own name.** Email carries your logo and comes from your own domain.

## How it goes.

For example, a founder sends a mutual NDA before a first call, the other side signs from a link, and both keep a copy with its certificate.

1. **Pick**: NDA template
2. **Send**: To the other side
3. **Sign**: Both sign
4. **Keep**: Certificate

## A copy you can stand behind.

**Sealed copy.** One PDF with every signature, sealed so a later change shows.

**Certificate.** Who signed, when and from where, on the last page.

**Audit log.** Each step in order, exportable as a CSV.

## Questions

**Who signs first?**

You choose. One at a time, or both together.

**Can the other side sign on a phone?**

Yes. They open the link and sign, with no account.

**Is the NDA legal advice?**

No. It is a starting point in plain words. Change what does not fit before you send it.
