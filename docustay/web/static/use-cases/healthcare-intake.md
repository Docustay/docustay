# Patient intake forms

Send the intake and consent forms ahead of the visit, so the first ten minutes are not paperwork.

## Flip through the pages first.

Each card is the real document: its pages, its fields, its wording.

- [Patient intake form](/templates/patient-intake-form)
- [Medical consent form](/templates/medical-consent-form)

## Two forms for before a visit.

**Patient intake form.** Contact details, history and what brings the person in.

**Medical consent form.** Agreement to care, signed and dated.

**Their language.** The person picks one of 14 languages for the signing page.

## How it goes.

For example, a clinic sends the intake and consent forms two days ahead, and the patient fills them in at home.

1. **Send**: Before the visit
2. **Fill in**: On a phone
3. **Consent**: Signed and dated
4. **Keep**: Certificate

## A copy you can stand behind.

**Sealed copy.** One PDF with every signature, sealed so a later change shows.

**Certificate.** Who signed, when and from where, on the last page.

**Audit log.** Each step in order, exportable as a CSV.

## Questions

**Is this a health-records system?**

No. Docustay is not for protected health information yet, so do not collect it through Docustay. It collects forms and signatures.

**Can a guardian sign for a child?**

Yes. Name the guardian as the signer.

**Who can see the documents?**

Only people in your workspace with the right role.
