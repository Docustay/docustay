# Your logo, your colour.

Show your logo and your colour on the signing page and the documents, add a welcome note, and send every email under your name with your reply-to address. It is on every plan.

## Four things that carry your name.

**Logo and colour.** Documents and the signing page carry your logo and your brand colour.

**Welcome note.** A short note at the top of the signing page, in your words.

**Your name on every email.** Every email goes out under your sender name, with your reply-to address, so replies come to you.

**Letterhead and address.** Your address line and footer text appear where a document needs them.

## It reads as coming from you.

The email, the signing page and the certificate show your name, your logo and your colour. A small line says the document was sent with Docustay.

**From.** Your sender name, with your reply-to address.

**Look.** Your logo and colour.

## Need no Docustay mark at all?

To embed Docustay in your own product, resell it or keep a private fork, you need a commercial licence, from about $3,000 a year. A self-hosted install can also remove the footer and send from its own domain, mailbox or mail server.

- The hosted service always shows the Docustay line on signed copies.
- Self-host: remove the footer yourself, and use your own mail server.

## Three steps to your own look.

**Add your logo.** Upload your logo under Settings, Branding, and pick your brand colour.

**Write a welcome note.** A line or two at the top of the signing page, such as who is asking and why.

**Send a test.** Send a document to yourself with a test key and check the email, the signing page and the certificate.

## What changes, and what stays Docustay's.

- **Email**: Your sender name and reply-to address on every email
- **Signing page**: Your logo, your colour and your welcome note
- **One document**: The subject and message of a single send, and the reply-to and sender name from the API

- **Hosted**: A small Docustay line on signed copies, on every plan
- **Seal**: The sealing certificate belongs to Docustay
- **Records**: The audit trail format does not change

## Embed it, or run it yourself.

**Embedded signing.** Show the signer inside your own product so there is no Docustay page at all.

**Self-host.** Run the whole product on your own server under the AGPL-3.0, with your own domain, mail server and footer. A commercial licence covers resale and a private fork.

## Questions

**Is branding on Free?**

Yes. Your logo, colour, welcome note, sender name and reply-to address are on every plan.

**Can I remove the Docustay footer?**

Not on the hosted service. A self-hosted install can, and a commercial licence covers a fully white-labelled product.

**Can I embed instead?**

Yes. Embedded signing shows the signer inside your own product.

**Does branding cost extra?**

No. Your logo, colour, welcome note, sender name and reply-to address are on every plan.

**Can one document have its own subject and message?**

Yes. On the Details step of a send you can change the subject and the message for that document alone, and from the API you can set the reply-to address and the sender name.
