# Pricing

- **Free**: 15 documents a month, 10 AI drafts, 5 new templates a month, 15 different signers a month, one team member. A 2% fee on payments taken while signing.
- **Pay as you go**: $5 per team member per month, signers free. 25 new templates a month. 15 documents free, then $0.50 each. 10 AI drafts free, then $0.30 each. Budget of $50 by default with alerts at 50, 80 and 100%.
- **Pro**: $39 a month for the whole workspace, unlimited team members, signers, documents and templates, 100 AI drafts a month, your own Plays and no fee on payments. Founders who subscribe by the cut-off date shown on the pricing page pay $25 a month while the subscription stays active.
- **Lifetime**: $299 once, for the first 200 workspaces. Unlimited documents, 50 AI drafts a month and everything in Pro, for as long as Docustay runs the hosted service.
- **Self-host**: free under the AGPL-3.0. Self-host Pro is $390 a year per install, for your own business.
- **Commercial licence**: from about $3,000 a year, to embed, white-label, resell or keep a private fork.

See [licensing](/licensing) and [self-host](/self-host).
