# Pricing

- **Free**: 15 documents and 5 new templates a month (documents and forms together). Forms, Scribe, API, webhooks and embedding included.
- **Pro**: $39 a month for the whole workspace, unlimited users, documents and templates. Founders who subscribe by the cut-off date shown on the pricing page pay $25 a month while the subscription stays active.
- **Self-host**: free under the AGPL-3.0. Self-host Pro is $390 a year per install, for your own business.
- **Commercial licence**: from about $3,000 a year, to embed, white-label, resell or keep a private fork.

Payments in a document: 2% on Free and 0% on Pro, on top of Stripe's fees. See [licensing](/licensing) and [self-host](/self-host).
