title: Free form templates: one link, many responses

---

# Free form templates

- [Contact and inquiry](/form-templates/contact-and-inquiry): A short contact form: who is asking, how to reply, what it is about and the message.
- [Event registration](/form-templates/event-registration): Register for an event: attendee details, sessions or tickets, dietary and access needs, and agreement to the event rules.
- [RSVP](/form-templates/rsvp): Reply to an invitation: attending or not, how many, meal choice and a note for the host.
- [Volunteer application](/form-templates/volunteer-application): Apply to volunteer: interests, availability, experience, references and agreement to the volunteer code.
- [Job application](/form-templates/job-application): Apply for a job: contact details, the role, right to work, experience, education, availability and references.
- [New hire information](/form-templates/new-hire-information): A new team member's contact details, preferred name, emergency contact, uniform and equipment needs and first-day questions.
- [Emergency contact](/form-templates/emergency-contact): Who to call in an emergency: two contacts, how they are related and the best way to reach them.
- [Customer feedback survey](/form-templates/customer-feedback-survey): Rate the experience, say what went well and what did not, and choose whether to be contacted.
- [Order form](/form-templates/order-form): Place an order: items and quantities, delivery or collection, and agreement to the terms. No payment details are collected.
- [Change of contact details](/form-templates/change-of-contact-details): Update a name, email, phone or address, with the date the change takes effect.
- [Appointment request](/form-templates/appointment-request): Request an appointment: the service, preferred dates and times, how to reach you and anything to prepare.
- [Room or facility booking request](/form-templates/facility-booking-request): Request a room or facility: date and times, the purpose, the number of people, the layout and equipment, and agreement to the house rules.
- [Suggestions and ideas](/form-templates/suggestions-and-ideas): Share an idea: what it is, the problem it solves, who it helps and whether you would like to help make it happen.
- [Vehicle inspection checklist](/form-templates/vehicle-inspection-checklist): A pre- or post-use check of a vehicle: mileage, fuel, lights, tires, fluids, damage, with photos and a supervisor's sign-off.
- [Incident report](/form-templates/incident-report): What happened, when and where, who was involved, any injury or damage, what was done straight away, with witnesses and photos, reviewed by a supervisor.
- [Time-off request](/form-templates/time-off-request): Request time off: type of leave, dates, hours, cover arrangements, approved by a manager.
- [Expense reimbursement](/form-templates/expense-reimbursement): Claim back expenses: each item, date, amount and purpose, with receipts attached, approved by a manager. No bank details are collected.
- [Purchase request](/form-templates/purchase-request): Ask to buy something: what, from whom, why, how much and by when, with a quote attached and an approver's signature.
- [Vendor information](/form-templates/vendor-information): A supplier's business name, contacts, what it supplies, insurance and certifications, with documents attached. No bank or tax numbers are collected.
- [Membership application](/form-templates/membership-application): Apply to join: contact details, membership type, interests, how you heard about us and agreement to the rules, approved by the membership secretary.
- [Donation pledge](/form-templates/donation-pledge): Pledge a gift: the amount, how often, when, how to be thanked and whether the gift may be named. No card or bank details are collected.
- [Service request or work order](/form-templates/service-request-work-order): Request work: what needs doing, where, how urgent, access arrangements, photos, and the technician's sign-off on completion.
- [Return and refund request](/form-templates/return-and-refund-request): Ask to return an item: the order, the item, the reason, the outcome wanted and photos, approved by the seller. No card details are collected.
- [Maintenance request](/form-templates/maintenance-request): Report a repair: what and where, how urgent, permission to enter, photos, and sign-off when fixed.
- [Move-in and move-out condition checklist](/form-templates/move-in-move-out-checklist): Room-by-room condition at move-in or move-out, meter readings, keys handed over and photos, signed by the occupant and the property manager.
- [Class or course registration](/form-templates/class-or-course-registration): Enroll in a class: the course and session, experience level, learning needs, emergency contact and the terms of the course.
- [Reference check](/form-templates/reference-check): A referee confirms how they know the candidate, the dates, the role, and gives an honest view of strengths, areas to develop and suitability.
- [Exit interview](/form-templates/exit-interview): A leaving team member's reasons, what worked, what could be better, and whether they would return, kept confidential.
- [Visitor sign-in](/form-templates/visitor-sign-in): A visitor's name, organization, who they are seeing, arrival time, vehicle and agreement to the site rules.
- [Policy acknowledgement](/form-templates/policy-acknowledgement): Confirm you have read a named policy and version, understood it, had the chance to ask questions and will follow it.
