# Whatever needs a signature

Pick the kind of paperwork you send. Each page shows the templates that fit and how it goes.

## Eight places people start.

- [Contracts that get signed](/use-cases/contracts): Send an agreement and let everyone sign from a link.
- [NDAs signed in minutes](/use-cases/ndas): Send a non-disclosure agreement before the first conversation.
- [Waivers and consent forms](/use-cases/waivers-and-consent): Share one link before an event or a visit.
- [HR paperwork and onboarding](/use-cases/hr-and-onboarding): Send the offer, then the first-day paperwork, to each new person as a package they can sign in one sitting..
- [Leases and rental applications](/use-cases/real-estate): Collect the application, then send the addendum and the deposit agreement.
- [Patient intake forms](/use-cases/healthcare-intake): Send the intake and consent forms ahead of the visit, so the first ten minutes are not paperwork..
- [Permission slips and school forms](/use-cases/education): Send a permission slip to every family at once.
- [Statements of work and client agreements](/use-cases/agencies): Send the scope, then each change to it, then the payment plan.

## Questions

**Is there a template for everything?**

No. There are 24. Describe a different document and Scribe writes it; you review it before it goes out.

**Is a template legal advice?**

No. It is a starting point in plain words.
