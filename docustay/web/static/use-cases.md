# Whatever needs a signature.

Pick the kind of paperwork you send. Each page shows the templates that fit and how it goes.

## Seven places people start.

- [Contracts that get signed](/use-cases/contracts): Send an agreement and let everyone sign from a link.
- [NDAs signed in minutes](/use-cases/ndas): Send a non-disclosure agreement before the first conversation.
- [Waivers and consent](/use-cases/waivers-and-consent): Send a waiver to each person before an event or an activity.
- [HR paperwork and onboarding](/use-cases/hr-and-onboarding): Send the offer, then the first-day paperwork, to each new person as a package they can sign in one sitting.
- [Leases and rental applications](/use-cases/real-estate): Collect the application, then send the addendum and the deposit agreement.
- [Permission slips and school forms](/use-cases/education): Send a permission slip to every family at once.
- [Statements of work and client agreements](/use-cases/agencies): Send the scope, then each change to it, then the deposit.

## Every use case is the same short path.

Pick a template or describe one. Send it to the people who sign. Each signs in turn or all at once, and everyone keeps the sealed copy.

**Start.** Pick from 54 built-in templates, or describe your own and review what Scribe builds.

**Sign.** No account for the signer, any device.

**Keep.** A sealed PDF and a certificate, with every step in the audit log.

## Pick the one closest to your job.

Each page names the templates for that work, what to check before you send, and what you keep afterwards.

**Contracts.** Services agreements, contractor agreements and the paperwork around a job. Two parties sign, in order or together, and both keep a sealed copy.

**NDAs.** A mutual NDA and a non-solicitation agreement for the start of a deal. Both sides sign from a link on any device, with no account.

**Waivers and consent.** Liability waivers, photo and media releases and permission slips, sent to each person on a list, with a record for everyone who signs.

**HR and onboarding.** Offer letters and a first-day pack the new person fills in on a phone before they arrive, with one signed copy for the file.

**Real estate.** Lease addenda, rental applications and deposit agreements, sent to each applicant or tenant on their own, with the deposit paid inside the document.

**Education.** Permission slips and consent paperwork sent to every family from a list, so you can see at a glance who has answered and who has not.

**Agencies.** Statements of work and change orders, so each job and each change has its own signed scope and price.

## The same sealed record, whichever one you use.

Every use case ends the same way, so you only learn it once.

**A sealed PDF.** When everyone has signed, the finished PDF is sealed with a digital signature over the whole file, so a later change shows.

**A certificate on the last page.** It lists who signed and when, and carries a hash of the file, so the copy can be checked on the verify page without an account.

**An audit trail you can export.** Every step on a document is recorded, from sent to opened to signed, and the trail exports as a CSV.

## Questions

**Is there a template for everything?**

No. There are 54 built-in templates. Describe a different document and Scribe writes it; you review it before it goes out.

**Is a template legal advice?**

No. It is a starting point in plain words.
