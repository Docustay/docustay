# Forms that collect and sign.

Share one link. People fill in your fields and sign in the same sitting, and each answer lands in your workspace.

## Three kinds of form.

**Link forms.** One address anyone can open. Each person who submits gets their own signed copy.

**Data forms.** Collect names, dates, choices and files with no signature at all, when all you need is the answers.

**Form templates.** Save a form once and reuse it for the next event, the next client or the next season.

## From a link to your list.

1. **Link**: Shared by you
2. **Fill in**: On any device
3. **Sign**: Typed or drawn
4. **Saved**: Sealed copy + record

## Everyone you collect is saved.

Names and emails from your forms join your People list, so the next send starts with a pick instead of typing.

- Open a person to see every document with them.
- Send a saved person a new document in two taps.

- **Upload**: 25 MB on Free, 100 MB on Pro
- **Languages**: 14 for the person signing
- **Account**: None needed to submit

## Forms people collect all the time.

Each of these is a built-in form template with its fields placed. Use one as a form, or change it first.

- [Photo release](/templates/photo-release-form): Permission to use photos and video of a person.
- [Permission slip](/templates/permission-slip-template): A parent or guardian agrees to a school trip.
- [Liability waiver](/templates/liability-waiver-template): A person agrees to the risks of an activity.

## Questions

**Can the same person submit twice?**

Yes. Each submission is its own document with its own sealed copy.

**Where do I see the answers?**

In the document list, one row for each submission, and in the People list.

**Can I take payment on a form?**

Yes. Add a payment field and it is charged through your own Stripe account.
