# Sign from any device.

A link opens the document itself, not a login screen. Fill in your fields, sign and finish. No account, no app.

## What the signer sees.

**The document.** The page opens on the document. A bar at the bottom counts the fields left and jumps to the next one.

**The signature.** Type a name, or draw it with a finger or a mouse. Tick that you agree to sign electronically, then finish.

**The copy.** When everyone has signed, each person receives the sealed PDF by email.

## Not everyone signs.

**Signer.** Fills in their own fields and signs.

**Approver.** Approves or rejects, with a comment.

**Viewer.** Opens the document and confirms they have read it.

**Assistant.** Fills in fields for someone else and saves them.

## Decline, ask, or come back later.

A signer can decline and say why, ask for changes, or save what they have filled in and return from the same link.

- An access code can be asked for before the document opens.
- A person who cannot sign can pass it on, and every pass is written into the record.
- The page, the emails and the certificate come in 14 languages, and Arabic reads right to left.

## Questions

**Does the signer need an account?**

No. They open the link and sign.

**Can they change someone else's fields?**

No. Only their own fields can be changed. The rest is read-only.

**Is the translation checked?**

The signing page, emails and certificate are machine-translated drafts in 13 languages, marked Beta, and English is the reference.
