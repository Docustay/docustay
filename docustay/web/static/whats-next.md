# What is next for Docustay.

Version 1 does one thing well: it gets your documents written, sent, signed and sealed, and it gives developers and self-hosters the whole platform. Here is what we are looking at for later versions. Nothing here is promised or dated; tell us what you would use first.

## Thirteen ideas. Five are next up.

**Reports.** Next up. See and export what was sent, signed and paid.

**Sign in person.** Next up. Kiosk mode and QR codes.

**Your own email domain.** Next up. Email from your own address.

**Public forms.** Next up. Share a link anyone can fill in and sign.

**Edit email wording.** Next up. Change the words of the emails Docustay sends for you.

**Automations.** Send the next document automatically when one is signed.

**More languages.** Signing pages, emails and certificates in other languages.

**Contract dates.** Key dates and renewal reminders.

**Smart fields.** Fields that appear on conditions, calculated fields and sections.

**Signing links on your domain.** Signers open links that carry your name.

**Payment plans.** Instalments and subscriptions inside a signed document.

**Custom roles and permissions.** Roles beyond owner, admin and member.

**Connections.** Cloud storage, calendars and chat apps.

## What you tell us decides the order.

We build what people use. Every message to hello@docustay.app is read, and the ideas that come up most often move up.

## Questions

**Are these promised?**

No. Nothing on this page is promised or dated. It is a list of what we are looking at.

**Can I ask for something that is not here?**

Yes. Write to hello@docustay.app and say what you would use it for.

**Is anything here in the product today?**

No. Everything listed is not part of version 1.
