# New hire paperwork

One packet for a new starter, in several parts: welcome, handbook, confidentiality, equipment, payroll details and first-day information.

Use it on or before the first day, so the paperwork is not the first day. It gathers the usual forms into one send.

## Who signs

- Company
- New starter

## Before you send

- Remove what you do not use.
- Add your own policies as extra pages.
- Keep tax and identity forms in your own payroll system.

## The facts

- 6 pages
- 50 fields
- About 16 minutes to fill in
