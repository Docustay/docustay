# Permission slip template

A parent or guardian gives permission for a child to take part in a trip or activity, with health notes and emergency contacts.

Use it for a trip, an event or an activity that needs a parent's yes. It names the activity, the date and who to call.

## Who signs

- Parent or guardian

## Before you send

- Give the date, place and times.
- Add an emergency contact field.
- Say what the school or group will and will not cover.

## The facts

- 2 pages
- 20 fields
- About 8 minutes to fill in
