# Class or course registration

Enroll in a class: the course and session, experience level, learning needs, emergency contact and the terms of the course.

Enroll students with everything you need before the first class.

## About you

- Full name (text)
- Email (email)
- Phone (phone, optional)

## The course

- Course (text)
- Start date (text)
- Day and time (text, optional)
- Experience (choice)
- Goals (text, optional)

## Participant

- Participant's name (text, optional)
- Age group (choice)
- Needs (text, optional)
- Emergency contact (text)
- I have read the course terms, including attendance, cancellations and refunds. (tick box)
- If the participant is under 18, I am their parent or guardian and I agree for them to take part. (tick box, optional)
- I understand that what I enter here is stored by the organization that published this form and used only for the purpose (tick box)

## Signature

- Student signature (signature)
- Student date signed (date signed)
