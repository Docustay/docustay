# Rental application form

An applicant gives their details, address and income history, references and permission for the landlord to check what they have written.

Use it when someone wants to rent from you. It collects the details you need to decide.

## Who signs

- Applicant

## Before you send

- Ask only what the law lets you ask.
- Say whether a fee is charged.
- Tell applicants how long you take to decide.

## The facts

- 4 pages
- 43 fields
- About 18 minutes to fill in
