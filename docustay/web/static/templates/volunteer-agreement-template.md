# Volunteer agreement template

An agreement between an organization and a volunteer: the role, what each side commits to, confidentiality, safety, and how it ends.

Use it when someone will help at your school, club or event. It states what they will do and the rules they accept.

## Who signs

- Organization
- Volunteer

## Before you send

- Describe the role and the hours.
- List your safety and conduct rules.
- Say that the role is unpaid.

## The facts

- 3 pages
- 23 fields
- About 7 minutes to fill in
