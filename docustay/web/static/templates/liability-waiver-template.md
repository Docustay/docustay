# Liability waiver template

A participant acknowledges the risks of an activity, agrees to follow the rules and confirms their emergency contact.

Use it before a class, a race, a rental or any activity with risk. It states the risks and asks each person to accept them.

## Who signs

- Participant

## Before you send

- Describe the real risks of your activity.
- Check what your state allows a waiver to cover.
- Collect a guardian's signature for anyone under 18.

## The facts

- 2 pages
- 9 fields
- About 4 minutes to fill in
