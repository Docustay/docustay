# Event registration

Register for an event: attendee details, sessions or tickets, dietary and access needs, and agreement to the event rules.

Know who is coming, what they chose and what they need, before the day.

## About you

- Full name (text)
- Email (email)
- Phone (phone, optional)

## Your registration

- Organization (text, optional)
- Ticket type (choice)
- Sessions (choose any, optional)
- Guests (text, optional)

## So we can look after you

- Dietary needs (text, optional)
- Access needs (text, optional)
- Emergency contact name and phone (text, optional)
- I will follow the event's rules and the instructions of the organizers and venue staff. (tick box)
- I understand that general photographs of the event may be taken. I will tell the organizers on the day if I do not want  (tick box, optional)
- I understand that what I enter here is stored by the organization that published this form and used only for the purpose (tick box)

## Signature

- Attendee signature (signature)
- Attendee date signed (date signed)
