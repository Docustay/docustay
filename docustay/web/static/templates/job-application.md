# Job application

Apply for a job: contact details, the role, right to work, experience, education, availability and references.

Receive every application in the same shape, so candidates are compared fairly.

## About you

- Full name (text)
- Email (email)
- Phone (phone, optional)
- Street address (text)
- City (text)
- State or region (text)
- ZIP or postal code (postal code)

## The role

- Position (text)
- Type of work (choice)
- Available from (text)
- Right to work (choice)

## Experience

- Most recent employer and role (text, optional)
- Dates (text, optional)
- Main duties (text, optional)
- Other experience (text, optional)

## Education and skills

- Highest qualification (text, optional)
- Skills and certificates (text, optional)
- CV or résumé (file (PDF or picture))
- Cover letter (file (PDF or picture), optional)

## References

- Reference one (text)
- Reference two (text, optional)
- The employer may contact my references if I am shortlisted. (tick box)

## Adjustments

- Adjustments (text, optional)
- The information I have given is true and complete to the best of my knowledge. (tick box)
- I understand that what I enter here is stored by the organization that published this form and used only for the purpose (tick box)

## Signature

- Applicant signature (signature)
- Applicant date signed (date signed)
