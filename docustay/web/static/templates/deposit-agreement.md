# Deposit agreement

A customer pays a deposit to hold a booking, item or place: what it holds, when it is refunded, when it is kept, and the card payment.

Use it when you take a deposit for a rental, a booking or a job. It states the amount and the conditions for returning it.

## Who signs

- Business
- Customer

## Before you send

- Write the amount and what it secures.
- Say when and how it is returned.
- List what may be deducted.

## The facts

- 3 pages
- 18 fields
- About 7 minutes to fill in
