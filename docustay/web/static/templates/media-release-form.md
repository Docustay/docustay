# Media release form

A parent or guardian chooses where photos and video of their child may be used, with safeguards and a clear way to withdraw.

Use it when the person in the picture or recording is under 18. A parent or guardian signs on the child's behalf.

## Who signs

- Parent or guardian

## Before you send

- Say which media: photo, video or audio.
- List where it will be shown.
- Allow the guardian to withdraw permission later.

## The facts

- 3 pages
- 19 fields
- About 8 minutes to fill in
