# Medical consent form

A client agrees to a service after reading what it is, what to expect, how their information is handled and how to stop.

Use it before treatment starts. It records that the person understood and agreed.

## Who signs

- Client
- Provider

## Before you send

- Describe the care in words the person understands.
- Name who may sign for a minor.
- Check any extra consent your field requires.

## The facts

- 3 pages
- 22 fields
- About 9 minutes to fill in
