# Equipment loan agreement

A lender lends an item to a borrower: description and condition, how it may be used, return date and who pays if it is lost or damaged.

Use it when you lend equipment to a person or a team. It lists each item and the date it returns.

## Who signs

- Lender
- Borrower

## Before you send

- List each item with its condition.
- Set the return date.
- Say who pays if it is lost or damaged.

## The facts

- 3 pages
- 24 fields
- About 9 minutes to fill in
