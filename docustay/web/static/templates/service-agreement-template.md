# Service agreement template

A full agreement for work one person or business does for another: scope, fees, term, ownership, confidentiality, liability and how it ends.

Use it when you will do ongoing or repeated work for a client. It covers the scope, the fees, the term and how either side ends it.

## Who signs

- Provider
- Client

## Before you send

- Match the scope to what you actually offer.
- Write the fee and the payment date.
- Decide how much notice ends the agreement.

## The facts

- 8 pages
- 58 fields
- About 20 minutes to fill in
