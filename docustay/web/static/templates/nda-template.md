# NDA template

Two sides share private information and each agrees to protect the other’s: what counts, what they may do with it, how long it lasts.

Use it before you share plans, prices, code or client names with someone you may work with. It runs both ways, so each side is bound by the same promise.

## Who signs

- Party A
- Party B

## Before you send

- Name what counts as confidential, in your own words.
- Set how long the promise lasts.
- Check the state whose law should apply.

## The facts

- 3 pages
- 25 fields
- About 8 minutes to fill in
