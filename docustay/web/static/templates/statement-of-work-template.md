# Statement of work template

A detailed statement of work: objectives, scope, deliverables with dates, assumptions, acceptance, fees by milestone and change control.

Use it for one job under a larger agreement, so each job has its own scope. It fixes the deliverables, the dates and the price before work starts.

## Who signs

- Agency
- Client

## Before you send

- List each deliverable as something that can be checked.
- Give dates, not weeks.
- Say what happens to work outside the scope.

## The facts

- 6 pages
- 77 fields
- About 26 minutes to fill in
