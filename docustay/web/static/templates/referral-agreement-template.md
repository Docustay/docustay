# Referral agreement template

A company agrees to reward a referrer when a person they introduce becomes a customer: what counts, the reward, when it is paid and the rules.

Use it when someone will send you clients for a fee. It defines a referral and the payment.

## Who signs

- Company
- Referrer

## Before you send

- Define what counts as a referral.
- State the fee and when it is due.
- Say how long the arrangement lasts.

## The facts

- 3 pages
- 16 fields
- About 7 minutes to fill in
