# Non-solicitation agreement

An employee acknowledges and agrees not to solicit the company’s customers or colleagues for a set period after leaving.

Use it when two businesses will work closely and share staff or clients. It limits recruiting for a stated period.

## Who signs

- Company
- Employee

## Before you send

- Name who is covered: staff, clients or both.
- Set a period you could defend as reasonable.
- Check how your state treats these promises.
