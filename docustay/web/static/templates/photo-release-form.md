# Photo release form

Permission to take and use photos or video of a person, with choices about where they appear and a way to withdraw.

Use it before you publish a picture of someone. It says where and how the picture may be used.

## Who signs

- Subject

## Before you send

- List where the pictures will appear.
- Say how long you may use them.
- Use the media release form if the person is a minor.

## The facts

- 2 pages
- 16 fields
- About 7 minutes to fill in
