# Lease addendum template

An addendum that changes an existing lease on one subject, with its terms, start date and what stays the same, signed by both sides.

Use it when a lease needs one added term, such as a pet or a new occupant. It points at the lease and states the one change.

## Who signs

- Landlord
- Tenant

## Before you send

- Name the lease and its date.
- Write the added term plainly.
- Say that the rest of the lease still applies.

## The facts

- 3 pages
- 24 fields
- About 9 minutes to fill in
