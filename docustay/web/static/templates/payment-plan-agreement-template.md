# Payment plan agreement template

A customer agrees to pay an outstanding balance in installments: the amount, the schedule, what happens if one is missed, and the first payment.

Use it when a client will pay in parts. It lists each payment and its date.

## Who signs

- Business
- Customer

## Before you send

- Write each amount and its date.
- Say what happens when a payment is late.
- Connect your Stripe account to take a deposit as it is signed.

## The facts

- 3 pages
- 29 fields
- About 11 minutes to fill in
