# Vendor agreement template

An agreement under which a vendor supplies goods or services to a company: orders, delivery, pricing, quality, payment, insurance and ending.

Use it when you will buy from a supplier more than once. It sets deliveries, prices and payment dates.

## Who signs

- Company
- Vendor

## Before you send

- List what is delivered and how it is checked.
- State prices and payment terms.
- Say how either side can end it.

## The facts

- 6 pages
- 50 fields
- About 17 minutes to fill in
