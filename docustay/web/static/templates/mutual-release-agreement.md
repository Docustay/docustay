# Mutual release agreement

Two sides settle a matter and release each other from claims about it, with what is settled, any payment, and what is not released.

Use it to close out a dispute or a finished job. Each side gives up the claims named in it.

## Who signs

- Party A
- Party B
- Witness

## Before you send

- Name the claims being released.
- Say what, if anything, is paid.
- Check what your state does not allow a release to cover.

## The facts

- 3 pages
- 18 fields
- About 7 minutes to fill in
