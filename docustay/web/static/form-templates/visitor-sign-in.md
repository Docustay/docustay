# Visitor sign-in

A visitor's name, organization, who they are seeing, arrival time, vehicle and agreement to the site rules.

Know who is on site and that every visitor agreed to the rules.

## About you

- Full name (text)
- Email (email)
- Organization (text, optional)
- Phone (phone, optional)

## Your visit

- Person you are visiting (text)
- Reason (choice)
- Date (text)
- Arrival time (text)
- Vehicle (text, optional)
- Badge number (text, optional)

## Site rules

- I will follow the site's safety and security rules, stay with my host where asked, and sign out when I leave. (tick box)
- In an emergency I will follow staff instructions and go to the assembly point. (tick box)

## Signature

- Visitor signature (signature)
- Visitor date signed (date signed)
