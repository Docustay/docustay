# Volunteer application

Apply to volunteer: interests, availability, experience, references and agreement to the volunteer code.

Match volunteers to the right roles and times, with references to follow up.

## About you

- Full name (text)
- Email (email)
- Phone (phone, optional)
- Street address (text)
- City (text)
- State or region (text)
- ZIP or postal code (postal code)

## What you would like to do

- Areas of interest (choose any)
- Why you would like to volunteer (text)

## When you are available

- Days available (choose any)
- Times available (choose any)
- Earliest start date (text, optional)

## Experience and skills

- Relevant experience (text, optional)
- Languages (text, optional)
- CV or résumé (file (PDF or picture), optional)

## References

- Reference one (text)
- Reference two (text, optional)
- If I am accepted, I will follow the organization's volunteer code of conduct and its safety and privacy policies. (tick box)
- I understand that some roles need further checks before I start, and that the organization will explain any check and as (tick box)
- The information I have given is true and complete to the best of my knowledge. (tick box)
- I understand that what I enter here is stored by the organization that published this form and used only for the purpose (tick box)

## Signature

- Applicant signature (signature)
- Applicant date signed (date signed)

## For coordinator use

- Decision (choice)
- Note (text, optional)
- Coordinator signature (signature)
