How it works

  1. Upload a PDF (or start from a template) and drop signature, date and text fields on the pages.
  2. Add who signs and send. Signers use a link on their phone or computer; nobody needs an account.
  3. When everyone has signed you get one sealed PDF with a signing certificate and an audit trail.

Why people use it

Your document, unchanged

The signed result is the file you uploaded with the signatures added and sealed, not a retyped copy.

Proof that stands alone

Every signed record carries who signed, when, from where, and a seal that shows if a byte changes.

Hosted or yours
