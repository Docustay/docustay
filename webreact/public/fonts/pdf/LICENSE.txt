Used here, as plain WOFF (not WOFF2), specifically for server- and browser-side PDF
generation via @react-pdf/renderer — see src/money/invoicePdf.mjs's own header comment
and FINDINGS-OPEN.md F860 for why WOFF2 is not usable for this purpose (a real fontkit
